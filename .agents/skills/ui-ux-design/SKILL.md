# Evidence-led UI/UX design

**Trigger:** A specific interface, flow, visual direction or design review needs an actionable recommendation. Apply alongside the assigned role and lifecycle skills; this specialist skill supplies design reasoning, not authorization, a general investigation/decision record, or product implementation.

**Inputs:** Product goal and success signal; primary/secondary audiences and their actual or hypothesized tasks; brand voice, assets and constraints; platform (web/native/framework), devices, input modes and relevant assistive technologies; locales/scripts; existing UI/design system and research; content inventory, business/regulatory constraints, bandwidth/performance limits, target accessibility standard and level, available artifact (brief/mockup/prototype/production). Mark missing inputs as assumptions, not observed user needs.

**Procedure:**
0. **Establish contextual quality early.** Before material new UI, a materially different direction or substantial review, ask/validate a few discriminating questions: who uses it for which task and in what setting; on which platform/widths/input/AT; what desired polish, identity or taste would count as success; which current assets/system and liked/disliked references matter, and why? Include relevant content/locales and constraints from Inputs. Trace requested, sourced constraint, proposed/default, specifically agreed, open/unknown/declined and excluded positions to actual sources/review scope. External standards do not establish taste. Missing brand can use a labelled **proposed contextual default for visual review**, with alternatives/questions and reversal signals; do not treat it as agreed plain styling, omit previews or stop all work. Material missing audience/task/platform/priorities need the precise question, owner and bounded action for affected work.
   **Reuse/tiny basis:** inspect substantive adequacy of the current direction/design system and its exact assets/version, actual presentation/response and reviewed audience/tasks/platform/states/constraints. Reuse unchanged adequately reviewed input and add only gaps; a brand name, approved heading, root or stack approval is insufficient. A tiny in-direction label/focus/semantic-role correction can use concise sourced acceptance and meaningful focused checks, without two token alternatives or branding/style microapproval. A material new direction cannot take this route merely because few files change.
1. **Research before recommending.** Inspect task/user evidence and existing experience, then accessible primary standards for the *chosen platform and target*, host design-system/platform guidance and relevant user research. For each consequential recommendation record title, URL, publication/update date or unknown, edition/version, applicable context, exceptions and claim it supports. Recheck live sources if time-sensitive. See [source-to-rule map](references/evidence.md); open only relevant sections. Separate normative success criteria, informative explanation/implementation patterns, system conventions and aesthetic hypotheses. If primary material is inaccessible, do not invent its contents.
2. Map the journey: entry, decision, action, return, error, wait, empty and completion; identify needed information, terms, navigation model, content hierarchy and the most consequential failure. For color/branding, typography and layout propose roles (including contrast pairs, text styles, reading order and narrow/zoomed behavior), not universal hexes, type sizes or device breakpoints. For interaction/feedback specify pointer, keyboard, focus, state, content and motion alternatives. Account for translation, script/direction, slow connections and assistive technologies when relevant. Use [context checks](references/design-checks.md) on demand.
3. Compare **two plausible coherent contextual directions** for the same representative tasks/content/constraints (e.g. guided question flow vs grouped form; immersive editorial vs compact comparison). Make both substantive: hierarchy/layout/spacing/composition, semantic typography/color roles, primary versus secondary actions/navigation, interaction/feedback and responsive decisions. Show representative populated plus relevant error/empty/wait/completion states and meaningful narrow/wide treatment; label exact coverage and omissions. Two palette swaps are insufficient. Give each a fair best case, context/brand fit, benefits, accessibility risks and delivery effort. State a conditional recommendation *given the evidence*, where the alternative wins and what would reverse it. Rhythm, mood, distinctiveness and likely ease are expert aesthetic/usability hypotheses, not universal attractiveness or observed user preference.
   **Make actual visual previews:** use available suitable tools for concrete mockups/style illustrations/static frames for both directions; application implementation is unnecessary. Attached images/vectors with a usable view or a genuinely accessible rendered preview can suffice. Text wireflows, ASCII, source HTML/SVG/CSS, palette prose and artist descriptions are supplementary, never substitutes for viewable representations. Independently inspect decoded images or another actual usable visual view for legibility/content/coverage; retain drawing/source assets and exact rendered/presented asset identity and tool metadata. Label static illustrations as such, never imagined screenshots or executed interactions. A local file, internal tool read, generated asset, link or HTTP response does not prove the user can view it. Use only an available supported delivery route; no automatic install, public upload or paid provisioning. If that reviewing channel cannot receive/view the representation, presentation is **pending**, naming the exact prerequisite, owner and action.
   **Present and record scoped review:** assign a separately owned concise stable user-facing visual-review artifact plus exact asset paths, distinct from detailed report/compact receipt. Include sourced context/status, both directions/states/widths, tradeoffs/recommendation/proposed default, performed versus pending checks, hypotheses, prioritized selection/refinement questions and affected readiness. Through the orchestrator's full discussion route, require a successful worker report/receipt, actual answer-artifact read and substantive relay **with the real visual representations accessible to the reviewing user**. Preserve exact artifact version/statements, assets (path/version/hash or retained copy), coverage/platform and actual presentation/channel/reference plus supported access/viewability evidence. Private designer previews and relayed artist text fail this gate; claim actual user viewing only when response/evidence supports it.
   One assigned writer records the actual response/quote or stable reference as selected/refined/partial/open/pending/declined/conflicting with exact statement/asset/state/platform/version scope; preserve historical presented versions. Presentation of everything is not agreement; partial v1 review agrees neither unseen/rejected remainder nor v2 additions. Before affected material UI slice readiness/checkpoint or production, consume sufficiently reviewed direction/assets/constraints/response or valid reuse/tiny basis, tracing affected acceptance → reviewed statement/asset → fitting check → dependency/gap owner-action. Unavailable visuals stay pending even with adequate text. Discovery may finish a useful draft/open handoff; only affected dependencies pause, unrelated authorized work continues. Review grants no unnamed-child entry; material outcome/boundary/acceptance/platform/support/effort/cost or inherited changes follow D/revision/checkpoint gates. Routine in-scope refinements remain delegated.
4. Produce a buildable direction: information architecture and action priority; component/state inventory; tokens with foreground/background and measured contrast where claimed; typography scale by semantic role and locale coverage; behavior across available widths/inputs; error/success/wait/empty content; focus/keyboard/AT intent; motion/static path. Trace material choices to sources or label as local judgment. Do not copy another brand's palette or imply a design system is a web standard.
5. Check evidence proportionally. **Objective:** calculate contrast with actual rendered pairs; inspect applicable SC levels/exceptions; on a running artifact check semantic names/roles, keyboard/focus, reflow/zoom/text overrides, target bounds, locale changes, error recovery and motion. **Subjective:** review hierarchy, visual rhythm, consistency, distinctiveness, task clarity and brand fit as explicitly labeled expert judgment. Name unchecked outcomes and arrange representative task-based evaluation, including relevant language and AT users; neither a prose mockup nor a few tests demonstrate full conformance or actual usability.
   For dependent delivery, hand off exact reviewed assets/version/response, represented states/widths, hierarchy/type/color/layout roles, identity/action priority and interaction/responsive intent for **independent comparison of the stable delivered UI**; flag material unreviewed drift. Keep expert hypothesis, actual scoped preference, standards/tool measurements and actual participant/task/method/result study evidence individually identified. Mockup approval is not final rendered layout/geometry/keyboard/AT/reflow, full WCAG or user/learner satisfaction; screenshots do not establish required numeric rendered geometry, and contrast does not certify taste/usability. Use fitting functional/rendered/accessibility checks separately.

**Output:** Owned concise visual-review artifact and actual preview assets, separate detailed report and compact receipt: (1) constraints/source status/evidence/unknowns, (2) task/IA, (3) two viewable directions with state/width coverage, tradeoffs and conditional recommendation (or validated reuse/tiny basis), (4) buildable design specification with source-to-rule trace, (5) objective checks performed vs pending, (6) separate expert hypotheses, prioritized review/test questions and revision triggers, (7) exact presentation/actual scoped response or truthful pending handoff, affected readiness and independent later comparison basis. An authorized discovery specification can be provisional; dependent material production cannot treat it as reviewed. Prefer the smallest on-demand reference needed for the case.

**Stop/escalate:** A material accessibility/platform rule cannot be verified from applicable reachable sources, critical contextual requirements or sufficient visual access/review are missing for the affected commitment, source guidance conflicts with an inherited decision, or the design requires changing approved outcome/ownership. Record the precise question/prerequisite and owner/action; seek evidence or the applicable checkpoint for affected work. A local reversible hypothesis may be labeled provisional when the brief permits it; missing brand alone is not a blanket blocker or agreement on a default.
