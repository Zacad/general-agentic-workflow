# Endeavor requirements

**Trigger:** Substantial nonsoftware scope or a materially changing nonsoftware goal needs substantive discovery, scoped user review or validation of existing requirements before dependent delivery. Use `product-requirements` for software discovery. Tiny exact issues can use concise sourced coverage/readiness in the lifecycle brief/report. Broad substantial unresolved scope normally needs a separately reviewable specification/coverage artifact; a separate requirements unit is conditional on independent acceptance/dependency and semantic sizing, not a universal stage.

**Inputs:** Exact request and current answers/quotes or stable references; intended use and audience; existing specification and actual presented version/review responses; relevant credible sources and their freshness; unit/revision, applicable decisions and actual checkpoint where required; dependencies, owned specification/report paths and accessible own-unit investigator report for downstream work. Load `critical-reasoning` and the fitting entry/lifecycle skill. During bounded initial-root framing, clarify only enough for the first horizon; deeper work follows actual scoped authorization. This method does not itself authorize work, user agreement, child entry or unowned brief edits. Follow `.agents/workflow/protocol.md` for entry, source/status/review, coverage, sole-writer and revision/D/checkpoint gates.

**Procedure:**
Apply the protocol's applicable own-unit report-set/assigned planner integration gate to downstream work: actually consume relevant primary reports/material sources and their coverage, conflicts and dependency basis, not a summary/count assertion. Narrow contributions need not each cover all dimensions; the current applicable union must cover this commitment. Missing material facts/interactions require bounded same-unit renewal, retaining unaffected fresh evidence and linked replacements with gap owner/action. Full discussion, exact authority, scoped review and selected-level readiness remain separate gates.

1. **Recover the actual goal and review basis.** Ask what the result will help someone decide, do or learn, who will use it, and in what contexts. Derive representative scenarios and useful outcomes from the request. Compare plausible interpretations when they would lead to different deliverables; identify the consequence and ask the owner to resolve them. Read actual current sources, not just artifact labels. Record source/quote or reference, status and applicability for each material statement: requested, credibly sourced constraint, worker-proposed/default, specifically agreed, open/unknown/declined, or excluded. Distinguish sourced facts from user preference; preserve conflicting evidence. Exclusions without actual user/applicable approved-scope authority stay proposed; absence is a gap.
2. **Discover full breadth and useful depth.** Account for every requested material dimension, including difficult or not-yet-planned ones. For each, ask what content, comparisons, explanation or evidence the audience needs to achieve its useful outcome; what level of actionable detail, precision, confidence and source support would suffice; and what would be too shallow or misleading. Elicit required versus preferred constraints, dependencies and feasibility limits and how they affect scenarios. Ask what should happen for exceptional, unavailable, incompatible or uncertain-evidence cases, what must be explicitly outside scope, and which unresolved choices matter first. Prioritize questions by consequences/dependencies; give each alternatives, affected outcome, decision owner and bounded next action. Worker defaults are proposals. Do not replace the desired catalogue, course or other outcome with a conveniently available axis, offers or worker preference, or invent town/lesson/word/option quotas as substitutes for useful coverage.
   Adapt prompts rather than completing a rigid domain checklist. For a **course**, ask about audience/prerequisites, learning outcomes, requested curriculum axes/progression, and the desired explanation, examples, practice, solutions and learning checks and their depth. Specify expectations, not lessons/exercises/solutions. For a **trip**, first clarify whether the useful result is a catalogue, comparison, itinerary, lodging search or other purpose; then elicit its full requested geographic/experience/comparison/access/season/budget or other vectors and actionable detail. Availability evidence may constrain feasibility, but cannot silently redefine a desired catalogue as an offer list. These are examples, not a forced hierarchy or software architecture route.
3. **Validate reuse and choose a proportionate review artifact.** Compare existing input against the current goal, audience/scenarios/outcomes, full requested dimensions, constraints, depth/precision/evidence and exceptions; verify source freshness and actual specific review applicability separately. A generic approved root or “approved” heading is insufficient. Reuse adequate specifically reviewed current content by reference and add only missing/changed gaps. For broad substantial unresolved input, write a separately reviewable specification with concrete expected content/outcomes/depth, credible constraints, exceptions, exclusions with basis and prioritized open alternatives/owners. Keep proposals visibly separate. Use only owned paths; if a separate requirements outcome is independently acceptable/dependency-bearing, return it for parent preparation and a named checkpoint before its own investigator entry. Do not prebuild descendants.
4. **Make coverage actionable.** In the artifact or a linked map, trace every material requested dimension/specification statement through source/status/current review applicability → expected content/useful outcome/depth → current planned outcome or descriptive provisional later horizon → suitable failure-detecting acceptance/check → dependency/readiness, open gap owner/action, deferred disposition or explicit authorized exclusion basis. A label such as “access” or “practice” alone is not content/depth. Choose checks that could expose the actual failure: for example, scenario review can detect an unusable transport comparison or practice that cannot assess the agreed learning outcome, whereas counting headings cannot. Unplanned/deferred dimensions stay visible as requirements and gaps, not exclusions. Future horizons need no speculative IDs or complete hierarchy. Tiny exact issues can express this same meaningful trace in concise sourced brief/report prose with a focused check.
5. **Present precise review, retain truthful responses.** Hand the exact stable specification version/statements, proposals and prioritized questions to the orchestrator for explicit user review, with the owned artifact path and applicability. Retain what was actually presented, the actual response/quote or stable reference, and which statements/scope it addresses. Update only owned artifacts from actual responses. Adequacy, presentation, agreement and dependent readiness are separate claims: pending stays pending; partial review agrees only its specific statements; declined or conflicting choices remain so. Root approval covers its actually stated scope/defaults, not undisclosed additions. Unchanged specifically reviewed items and routine answers inside authorized scope need no ceremonial repeat approval. Material changes follow existing revision/D/checkpoint gates, through the assigned authority writer.
6. **Hand off readiness and limits.** Before dependent delivery preparation/production, trace each material acceptance item to sufficiently agreed current statements or actual applicable approved brief/D authority. Missing material coverage, agreement or feasibility keeps only affected dependent commitments provisional and blocks their checkpoint/entry/delivery and readiness claims; name the question, owner/action and unaffected authorized work that may continue. Specification review does not authorize an unnamed child or later material revision; every new child still needs its exact current-revision checkpoint before investigator entry, then its own distinct investigator. The authorized discovery outcome can finish as a useful draft/open review handoff without claiming downstream readiness. Report meaningful settled local choices, newly compared alternatives/rationale, scope and actual sources/authority for the sole brief writer's Decision log; do not create a duplicate authority tracker.

**Output:** Owned user-reviewable specification/coverage artifact or adequate reviewed-input reuse with gap additions (tiny exact issue: concise sourced brief/report statement); prioritized questions/alternatives/consequences/owners; exact presented review basis and actual response/applicability if available; criterion-level dependent readiness and honest pending limits in the owned report. End with affected **unit ID@revision** (or ID/revision to assign), what can advance or must wait, and **owner/action: orchestrator to present the exact specification/questions and obtain actual scoped review or request the applicable named/revised checkpoint when required; assigned writer to maintain authoritative state**. Return only the protocol's compact receipt.

**Preparation handoff:** adequate requirements feed appropriate domain structure, then the broad high-level horizon and explicit bounded delivery-group choice with the user per protocol. Propose project-specific taxonomy for understanding, never sizing proof, fixed levels or compulsory software architecture. Lead visible scope with outcome names/context before IDs/revisions. Supply coverage/readiness and retained deferral reasons, owners/actions, dependency impacts and reconsideration basis to the coordinating Work split/Relationships writer for sibling-level waves; do not generate the whole hierarchy. Authorized requirements/discovery/structure/preparation outputs establishing readiness are exempt from the dependent-delivery barrier, not from their own evidence/authority gates; an open specification remains an open handoff.

**Stop/escalate:** Missing/inapplicable own-unit evidence, contradictory material sources, essential unanswered/declined choice blocking dependent work, invalidated sizing, newly needed unowned paths, unclear authority or material boundary/inherited-choice change. Stop affected advancement and return the precise gap/owner/action through the existing investigation/preparation/authority route; continue only independent authorized work. Do not invent agreement, exclusions or dependent readiness to complete discovery.
