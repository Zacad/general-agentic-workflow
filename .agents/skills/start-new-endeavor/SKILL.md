# Start a new endeavor

**Inputs:** user's goal, initial constraints and sources, workspace instructions, allocated root/distinct attempt IDs, common bounded framing basis and separate owned report paths; for the separate writer, accessible own-root investigator report set and assigned brief path. No initial brief/checkpoint is required.

For the framing invocation/report steps below, apply the protocol's single/parallel/staged same-unit contract. The orchestrator explicitly assigns bounded questions, sources/applicability, dependencies/interactions and checks; no dimension units or worker delegation. One assigned planner actually reads applicable primary reports and material sources, integrating union coverage/conflicts/dependencies and whole-root sizing including no-split; narrow reports supply scoped implications/global limits, not each a full packet. Link primary reports/integration and selective retained/revalidated/replaced evidence. Missing material inputs stop reliant claims with owner/action, without weakening full discussion fail-closed, root-framing limits or selected-level readiness.

**Procedure:**
   **First-horizon visual framing:** for material interface-bearing goals, surface desired visual quality/taste and audience/tasks/use-setting/platform/current assets or references with actual source/status in the bounded investigation. Ask only material discriminating questions; expose gaps with question/owner/action. Route early to `ui-ux-design` so contextual alternatives and genuine viewable previews shape the authorized next horizon. Missing brand can be a labelled proposed contextual default for review, never agreed plain styling or permission to omit visuals. Framing need not finish branding/design before root approval; later sufficient scoped visual review or adequate reviewed reuse follows the protocol, without bypassing named-child entry.

1. Arrange the distinct investigator's bounded root-framing invocation with `investigate-unit` before substantive drafting. It clarifies desired outcome/success, inputs, constraints/exclusions and only enough evidence for the first horizon, writing its own report. The separate assigned writer reads that report and checks applicability per protocol; self-research/skill loading cannot replace entry. Mark assumptions rather than inventing requirements.
   For a substantial greenfield multi-feature product, distinguish the user's requested goal from worker-proposed defaults and open rules, endings, identity, saving/recovery, sharing and other consequential choices *as relevant to that product*. Surface their effects before build and ask a few discriminating questions now or assign detailed answers to a reviewable requirements child after root approval. For a checkers request, explicitly mark ruleset; resign, voluntary pass, no-legal-move loss, forfeit, agreed versus automatic draw; local/shared ranking and recovery as open until answered, including effects on saves/history/scoring. These are questions, not required features or assumed defaults. Adapt to other domains; leave deeper scenario discovery and architecture for the approved next horizon.
   For substantial nonsoftware framing, clarify actual goal/use, audience, requested material breadth, useful depth and exceptions enough to expose the first horizon's uncertainties. Use the protocol's source/status/review and coverage contract; do not substitute an available narrow axis or worker defaults for the requested outcome. Leave deeper discovery to `endeavor-requirements` within the authorized next horizon, or validate adequate specifically reviewed current input and add gaps instead of duplicating it.
2. Check for an existing root or incompatible workspace instructions. With assigned ownership, draft `.workflow/units/<id>/brief.md` from `.agents/workflow/unit.md` at revision 1, state `ready` only when the current scope/sizing is settled enough, linking the consumed framing report, with meaningful criteria and a check method. Frame the **intended outcome** so an eventual approval permits advancing toward it (directly or through approved children); do not accidentally define a framing-only root that forbids the intended work even after approval. State separately that no production is authorized *yet*.
   As sole brief writer, populate the mandatory `Decision log` per protocol from actual user requirements, applicable inherited constraints and settled local preparation/approach/check/dependency positions, with status, scope, alternatives, concise rationale and source/actual authority. Put proposals/open alternatives separately; use `unknown/not recorded` for missing historical options/rationale and label any new comparison or worker inference. Do not invent user reasoning or agreement. Check meaningful coverage before calling the draft complete; D/checkpoint summaries do not authorize work.
3. Populate the mandatory root `Work split` from consumed framing evidence per protocol: compare plausible semantic vectors including no-split, select with a unit-specific reason, and describe only current proposed child outcomes/dependencies/readiness/contribution/check horizons or a justified single-horizon leaf. Unresolved sizing stays provisional with question, evidence, owner and next action. Keep other branches provisional. If a consequential choice is already needed, propose a separate decision; do not mark it approved.
   For a nontrivial product needing reviewable discovery, identify a first **proposed** requirements child as a next action, not as already authorized; after the root's initial approval, prepare its named brief as parent work using `decompose-unit`. For broad unresolved nonsoftware scope, normally select a separately reviewable specification/coverage artifact before dependent content; propose a requirements child only when independently acceptable/dependency-bearing. A fully agreed small product or a book/trip may instead advance directly within its approved root **only when Work split justifies that leaf**, including why finer splitting adds coupling rather than useful independent review/check/retry, and current requirements/coverage and actual scoped agreement are adequate for that delivery. A tiny exact leaf needs only concise sourced coverage/readiness in its brief/report.
4. Hand the **ready brief's path and exact root ID@revision** to the orchestrator to present its *named scope* and explicitly request the initial user self-checkpoint. If no ID or brief was assigned, arrange that drafting first; do not end with only “proposal without approval.” Record only the user's actual answer in the root brief. No work within the root beyond framing proceeds before approval; root approval does not authorize a later requirements child. Request that child's separate named checkpoint before entering `product-requirements` or `endeavor-requirements`, including its own investigator first. Retain exact presented specification/statements and actual scoped review separately from adequacy/readiness per protocol. If a material answer is unanswered or declined, pause affected dependent delivery while independent authorized work may continue; discovery can still hand off its authorized draft/open review outcome.

**Output:** investigator's own framing report, then separate writer's root brief draft and attempt report with sources/unknowns; each returns a compact receipt per `.agents/workflow/protocol.md`. Initial approval alone does not require repeating applicable framing investigation.

**Horizon and selection:** first framing exposes meaningful preliminary high-level outcomes, useful proposed project-specific taxonomy/relationships and unresolved requirements/structure questions. After adequate requirements and appropriate structure (software architecture only when material), expose the broad horizon and explicitly choose the bounded delivery group with the user. Lead with understandable names/context, then exact IDs/revisions; selection may accompany a checkpoint but authorizes no unnamed unit. Persist selection and the next sibling preparation wave in existing Work split/Relationships per protocol, not a complete project tree. Before dependent delivery reconcile all selected paths to justified evidenced leaves or explicit deferred/blocked dispositions with reason, owner/action, dependency impact and reconsideration basis. Authorized discovery/requirements/structure/preparation outputs establishing readiness are exempt from that barrier; tiny justified no-split groups need no task tree. A direct approved leaf still consumes this selection/readiness basis.

**Stop/escalate:** ambiguous owner/goal, conflicting preexisting root, or missing constraint that changes the initial scope. Ask instead of approving or assuming.
