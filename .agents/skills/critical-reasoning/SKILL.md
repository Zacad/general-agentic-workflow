# Critical reasoning

**Inputs:** current question or unit brief, constraints, applicable decisions, inspectable sources, uncertainty.

**Procedure:**
1. Identify the claim or choice that matters to the next action; distinguish direct observation, sourced evidence, inference, and unknown. Do not manufacture citations or treat an agent report as proof without its evidence.
2. Verify material facts with the least costly credible check. For a consequential choice, identify its drivers and at least one plausible alternative; state why the preferred option better fits them and where it might fail.
3. Test inherited decisions against their stated assumptions. Reopen one deeply only for conflicting evidence, changed constraints, or a significant better alternative; do not gratuitously relitigate it.
4. Express confidence and limitations proportional to the evidence. Before claiming completion, compare the actual checked outcome to the unit's criteria.

**Output:** concise evidence/uncertainty and implications in the assigned report, decision, or coordination message; include source paths and failed checks where relevant.

**Stop/escalate:** material evidence is missing; an approved decision conflicts with findings; a consequential choice exceeds approved boundaries. Stop affected work and request a decision/checkpoint. Follow `.agents/workflow/protocol.md` for the authority and handoff rules.
