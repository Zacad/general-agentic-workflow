# Critical reasoning

**Inputs:** the question or item, applicable decisions, inspectable sources,
and the cost of being wrong.

## Procedure

1. Name the claim or choice that matters to the next action. Separate direct
   observation, sourced evidence, inference and unknown.
2. Check material facts with the least costly credible check. A file, a
   command, a version, a quote or a quick test beats memory.
3. For a consequential choice, name its drivers and at least one plausible
   alternative. Say why the preferred option fits better and where it may fail.
4. Test inherited decisions only against conflicting evidence, changed
   constraints or a materially better alternative. Do not relitigate settled
   choices out of habit.
5. Before claiming completion, compare the observed result to the item's
   acceptance criteria. A passing check does not cover an unrun criterion.

## Output

A short evidence statement in the report, decision or receipt: what is known,
what is inferred, what is unknown, and what it implies.

## Stop

Missing material evidence; an approved decision conflicts with findings; the
next step exceeds the approved scope. Return `needs-decision`.
