# Work-unit protocol (v1)

## Scope and state

One unit has a bounded outcome, explicit boundaries, acceptance criteria, inputs, and a way to check the result. A unit can prepare children or produce a leaf deliverable; neither is a mandatory level. A root can be an entire new endeavor **or** an existing project's bounded issue. Do not create a complete hierarchy in advance. Preparing a child brief is parent work; entering the child's investigation/production is child work.

Use unique IDs within the workspace (`U-0001`, `D-0001`, `A-0001`). The orchestrator allocates unit and decision IDs; a delegated worker can propose an ID but must not race another worker for it. Store authoritative briefs at `.workflow/units/<unit-id>/brief.md` and decisions at `.workflow/decisions/<decision-id>.md`. A unit's material `Revision` starts at 1. Bump it for changes to outcome, boundaries, acceptance criteria, or consequential inherited choices; editorial changes do not bump it. Keep a brief's old checkpoint entries when editing it. Assign a single writer for each brief/decision at a time.

Unit states: `draft` (still being framed), `ready` (prepared for a checkpoint), `active` (authorized work underway), `blocked` (cannot advance pending evidence, dependency, or checkpoint), `verified` (outcome checked and reconciled), `stopped` (withdrawn or replaced). State alone **never** grants authorization. A brief may be ready but unapproved; approval of a brief is distinct from verification of its outcome. A failed check moves work back to active or blocked. A parent must reconcile its *own* outcome, including its selected children, before becoming verified. Its untouched provisional branches need not be completed unless its criteria require them.

## Checkpoints and revisions

The parent brief has an append-only `Checkpoints` section containing **only actual approvals**; keep preparation notes under relationships/evidence. For a root, record its initial self-approval in its own brief. An entry names every authorized unit and its material revision, the scope of authorization (advance within that brief), conditions, and the user approval's date and exact quote or stable conversation reference. A checkpoint may name a group, but covers no unnamed children and no later material revisions. Record approval only from the user's explicit response to the stated scopes, not silence, a worker recommendation, or a generic approval of a different plan. The orchestrator requests the checkpoint; it can record that bookkeeping itself or appoint one state writer.

Root drafting starts with bounded distinct investigation before first approval (see Unit entry). Once a unit has a checkpoint, its ordinary investigation, local design, production, and checking proceed without separate phase approvals. A material revision to an approved unit suspends advancement of that unit and affected descendants until a new checkpoint; unrelated branches keep their prior authorization. If the requested revision would contradict an inherited consequential choice, escalate to that decision's scope first. When entering a child, look up the parent's checkpoint for that child's current revision. Do not treat tool-level permission prompts as workflow checkpoints.

## Unit entry and evidence applicability

Every actual new outcome unit starts with a **distinct investigator invocation**, producing an accessible own-unit attempt report before substantive downstream planning/production. A role, phase, retry or attempt is not a new unit; calling an investigator creates no investigation child or investigator-for-investigator chain. Investigators analyze their assigned task and evidence within that invocation. An independently useful research outcome may be a unit, subject to the same sizing and authority rules.

- **Initial root:** without a brief or checkpoint, the caller supplies an allocated root/attempt ID, bounded goal, constraints/exclusions, sources, framing basis and owned report path. The investigator checks that basis and investigates only enough to frame the root. A separate assigned writer reads the report and drafts the intended-outcome brief; no nonexistent brief/checkpoint is required for this initial investigation or framing. Link the report in the resulting brief. Advancement beyond framing still needs the root's initial named-revision approval.
- **Child:** parent investigation supports parent preparation, not child entry. Only after a parent checkpoint names the child's current revision does that child invoke its own investigator. It may reuse parent research after validating source identity/relevance and child-specific questions, assumptions, dependencies, sizing and checks; a parent report alone is not the child's investigation.
- **Consumption:** the caller confirms report existence/accessibility and own-unit investigator identity, passes its link and applicability basis, and routes substantive evidence disputes to a worker. The downstream planner/producer reads it and checks coverage of current scope, criteria, inputs, applicable decisions, material assumptions/dependencies and source volatility before work. A receipt, orchestration assertion, skill label, self-research or wrong-unit report cannot substitute. Missing/inapplicable evidence closes this gate; stop affected downstream work and arrange renewed distinct same-unit investigation. Later local research is allowed after the gate.
- **Continuation:** reuse applicable fresh same-unit evidence on session/role changes and routine retries. Neither age alone, editorial change nor initial approval requires another investigator. Missing/inaccessible/wrong-unit reports, uncovered or contradicted material evidence, or changed material uncertainty/dependencies require proportionate renewed investigation **in the same unit**. Retain old reports and link revalidation/replacement with what remains applicable. Refreshing evidence never grants a revised checkpoint or overrides an applicable decision.

The entry packet covers authority (or root-framing basis), material evidence and counterevidence, questions/unknowns, coverage/dependencies, approach/sizing candidates and falsifiable checks/review blockers. Use `investigate-unit` for the method. A tiny coherent leaf may answer these in a short paragraph with real sources; no heavyweight template or phase tree is required.

## Work split and recursive sizing

Every new/prepared and prospectively materially revised brief must contain a meaningful `Work split`; historical briefs need no retrofit. Compare plausible **semantic candidate vectors, including no-split**, from current outcome/dependency evidence. State the selected vector and unit-specific reason with source links. A tiny comparison may be short sourced prose rather than tables; placeholders or “small enough” are not assessment. The assigned brief writer records meaningful settled split/dependency choices in the existing Decision log, keeping proposals separate.

Favor useful independent boundaries: observable completion, distinct user review/acceptance, material choice/uncertainty, evidence/verification needs and isolated failure/retry, with explicit dependencies and settled output ownership. Shared sources, research or runtime do not erase independently acceptable outcomes. Do not substitute domain hierarchies, count thresholds, role/phase recursion or file/technical-layer fragments for outcomes. A coherent intermediate capability spanning files can be a leaf with honest deferred limits and an adequate observable result/check.

- **Children selected:** describe each current proposed child's bounded outcome/boundaries, material inputs/dependencies, readiness questions with owner/action, contribution to parent criteria, and completion/review/check horizon. Prepare only the next useful set, keeping later branches provisional. Distinguish provisional proposals, ready source-checked briefs and actually authorized children; unresolved material commitments keep dependent children out of checkpoint requests. Preparation is parent work; entering any child still requires its exact ID/current revision explicitly checkpointed in the parent after preparation.
- **No children selected:** identify this unit's single adequate observable completion/review/check horizon, manageable uncertainty/dependencies, and specifically why finer splitting adds coupling rather than useful independent acceptance/check/retry. Few files, one producer or shared resources alone cannot justify a leaf.
- **Sizing unresolved:** keep it explicitly provisional with the sizing question, evidence needed, responsible owner and bounded next action. “Split unknown” never establishes leaf or readiness.

Reassess recursively from actual unit evidence in **each distinct own-unit investigator invocation**: initial-root investigation frames the first horizon; every authorized new child's investigator tests meaningful finer boundaries and its proposed leaf rationale, validating reused parent inputs. Parent preparation records each child's reasoned Work split without entering it or pretending it has already had its own investigation. Recommend bounded further preparation when independently useful outcomes exist; do not prebuild a broad hierarchy or invoke unapproved descendants to settle sizing. An invocation is not another unit.

Producers consume the **current justified leaf** and applicable own-unit investigator report before delivery; a leaf label is insufficient when evidence contradicts it. Changed scope/evidence, uncertainty or dependencies that invalidate sizing stop affected delivery and return through proportionate same-unit investigation, assigned planning/preparation and reconciliation, retaining prior evidence/rationale and identifying impacts. Producers do not silently create/enter children or edit unowned briefs. Preparing children is not automatically a material parent revision; actual outcome/boundary/criterion or consequential inherited change is, suspending affected advancement for matching revised approval (and decision-scope resolution when needed). Refreshed evidence supplies no authority; unrelated authorized work may continue.

## Requirements, review and coverage

Use `product-requirements` for substantive software discovery and `endeavor-requirements` for substantial nonsoftware or materially changing nonsoftware goals. Broad substantial unresolved scope normally needs a separately user-reviewable specification/coverage artifact before dependent delivery. A separate requirements **unit** is conditional on independent acceptance/dependency and semantic sizing, not required for every tiny leaf. A tiny exact issue can use a concise sourced coverage/readiness statement in its brief/report. Apply the existing entry, ownership and authority gates to either route; specialist methods grant no authority themselves.

Trace material statements to actual sources, status and current applicability: **requested**, **credibly sourced constraint**, **worker-proposed/default**, **specifically agreed**, **open/unknown/declined**, or **excluded**. Link the current request/quote, evidence or actual scoped review reference as appropriate; preserve conflicting sources. External evidence does not establish user preference. An exclusion needs actual user or applicable approved-scope authority; otherwise it remains proposed. Missing dimensions are gaps, not exclusions. Reuse existing input only after checking substantive adequacy for the current goal, audience/scenarios, full requested dimensions, constraints, useful depth/precision/evidence and exceptions, source freshness and actual specific review applicability. An “approved” heading or generic root approval proves neither adequacy nor agreement; reference adequate reviewed content and add only missing/changed gaps.

Separate **adequacy, presentation, agreement and dependent readiness**. Explicitly present the specification/proposals for review; retain the exact artifact version/statements presented, actual response/quote or stable reference, and the scope to which that response applies. Keep pending, partial, declined and conflicting review truthful: partial agreement covers only its reviewed statements. Root approval covers actually stated scope/defaults, not undisclosed additions. A discovery outcome may complete as a useful draft/open review handoff without claiming dependent readiness. Unchanged specifically reviewed input and ordinary answers within authorized scope need no ceremonial repeat review or phase checkpoint.

Cover **every material requested dimension**, including difficult, unplanned or deferred ones, using a linked map or equivalent concise prose:

`dimension / specification statement → source/status/current review applicability → expected content, useful outcome and depth → current planned outcome or descriptive provisional horizon → suitable failure-detecting acceptance/check → dependency/readiness, open gap with owner/action, deferred disposition, or explicit authorized exclusion with basis`.

A dimension label or generic quality check is insufficient. Deferral retains the requirement and its readiness gap; it is not an exclusion. Do not preallocate a full hierarchy or future IDs. Before dependent delivery preparation/production, consume this trace and tie each material acceptance item to sufficiently agreed current statements or actually applicable approved brief/D authority, not narrative assumptions. Missing material coverage, agreement or feasibility keeps affected dependent commitments provisional and blocks their checkpoint/entry/delivery and readiness claims; identify question, owner and bounded next action. Unrelated authorized work may continue. Review is not a checkpoint for an unnamed child: each new child still needs its named current revision approved **before investigator entry**, then its own distinct investigator. Material changes follow the existing revision/D/checkpoint gates. One assigned brief writer consumes sources and worker reports for the Decision log; the specification/map is evidence, not a competing authority store.

## Testing strategy and scoped review

Before affected substantial software delivery with material behavior/quality/risk testing commitments, create or validate a feasible, sufficiently reviewed strategy and critical coverage. Use `code-architecture` when architecture matters, otherwise the conditional testing method in `choose-approach` for existing software. Compare at least two implementable strategies for the **same sourced normal, error, recovery and quality scenarios**, with confidence/missed failure surfaces, setup/maintenance/human/cost, test-feedback latency, determinism/flakiness and runtime/service/device prerequisites; a library list or evidence ladder alone is insufficient. Trace each material scenario/acceptance to source/status/current review, concrete state/input → method/check level → observable expectation, evidence/limits, version-scoped primary sources and separately observed local capability, planned-unrun/blocked or separately linked exact executed result, and affected dependency/gap owner/action or authorized exclusion. See the existing choice method and architecture reference for detail.

Assign a worker a separately owned **concise user-facing strategy artifact**, distinct from its detailed report and compact receipt. Through the orchestrator's full discussion/answer-artifact route, actually read/relay its drivers, alternatives/recommendation, critical coverage/limits, prerequisites and prioritized questions before affected material commitments. Retain exact stable version/statements presented, presentation reference, actual response/reference and scoped applicability through owned artifacts and the assigned writer. Stored unseen strategy, generic architecture/stack approval, partial response or v1 review cannot agree unpresented/rejected statements or v2 additions. Apply the existing adequacy/presentation/agreement/readiness and D/revision/checkpoint gates; only affected dependencies wait. Review neither authorizes unnamed children nor executes tests. Feasible agreed future tests can remain planned-unrun before code exists; an independent verifier may add necessary failure-detecting checks beyond the plan.

Reuse adequate specifically reviewed unchanged strategy with its exact scope/response; add only gaps. A tiny focused existing issue with exact approved acceptance, meaningful regression coverage and honest limits may use concise sourced brief/report adequacy, without token alternatives, forced PRD/architecture/strategy unit or runner microapproval. A separate strategy outcome is conditional on independent acceptance/dependency; initial root framing need not settle it. Executable teaching uses fitting reconstruction/runtime/correct-reason checks and learner-effectiveness limits; pure prose/travel uses domain sources/scenarios/usefulness checks, not artificial software tests. Do not invent features, targets, benchmarks, support cuts or installations to claim feasibility.

## Visual direction and scoped review

For material new UI, a materially different visual direction or substantial interface review, surface desired visual quality/taste and audience/task/platform/brand/use-context with source/status early; load `ui-ux-design` before affected direction production. That specialist supplies the detailed method: two substantive contextual directions for the same representative tasks, states and narrow/wide treatment, with genuine viewable previews, tradeoffs and a conditional recommendation. Missing brand may use a labelled **proposed contextual default for review**, not agreed plain styling or omitted visuals; material context gaps need question/owner/action.

Assign a separately owned concise stable visual-review artifact and exact assets, distinct from report/receipt. Use the orchestrator's full discussion/answer-read/relay route to actually deliver accessible visual representations. Retain exact artifact statements/version, source and presented asset identities, states/widths/platform, actual channel/presentation reference and supported viewability evidence; a private image, textual layout, ASCII, raw HTML/SVG/CSS, artist description, file/link or HTTP success alone cannot substitute. Without positive delivery evidence, presentation stays pending with owner/action. One assigned writer retains historical presented versions and the actual selected/refined/partial/open/declined/conflicting response/reference with exact applicability; full presentation is not agreement, partial v1 response covers neither its remainder nor future v2.

Before affected material UI readiness/checkpoint or production, consume sufficient actually reviewed direction/assets/constraints/response and acceptance-to-review/check/dependency trace. Validate adequate specifically reviewed unchanged direction/design-system reuse against the current audience/tasks/platform/states and exact assets/version/presentation/response; add only gaps. A tiny in-direction label/focus/semantic-role correction uses concise sourced acceptance and focused checks, without token alternatives, brand workshops or style microapproval. File count does not make a material new direction tiny. Useful authorized discovery may finish an open handoff; only affected dependencies wait, unrelated authorized work continues. Review authorizes no unnamed child; own investigation and matching named revision/D/checkpoint gates still apply, while routine in-scope refinements remain delegated.

Hand off exact reviewed direction/assets/states/widths/semantic roles/action priority for independent later comparison of stable delivered UI, flagging material unreviewed drift. Keep aesthetic expert hypotheses, actual user preference, objective functional/rendered/accessibility measurements and actual participant/task/method/results usability evidence separate. Mockup selection proves neither final rendered geometry/keyboard/AT/reflow, full WCAG conformance nor user/learner outcomes; fitting execution checks remain required. Do not provision tools/services, upload publicly or incur paid commitments to invent preview feasibility.

## Independent outcome proof and user visibility

Before outcome reconciliation, assign an **actual distinct independent verifier invocation** against the exact authorized unit/revision and stable named target/producer attempt. Supply approved criteria, applicable requirements/decisions and exact current review statements/version/response, own-unit investigator report and applicability basis, source/variant manifest, published instructions/environment, and separately owned verifier report, result-artifact and concise user-summary paths. Retain the real invocation/session identity (or explicitly unavailable session metadata), actual own report and compact receipt; a role label, loaded skill, producer selfcheck or invented report is not an independent check. Investigators/verifiers write only their own attempts, never delivered solutions or authoritative state. Keep sources frozen during checking; a changed output requires a new stable identity and affected independent checks, preserving prior findings.

For **each material criterion**, derive a falsifiable expected result from actual approved requirement/brief/D/review authority and applicable **version-scoped primary sources**, independently of producer conclusions or an implementation-computed oracle. Record a concise linked proof trace:

`criterion + actual source/current approval/review applicability → independent expected-behavior basis → stable target/revision/published variant/source identity + producer attempt → actual investigator and distinct verifier invocation/report/receipt → failure hypothesis + method → environment/runtime/framework/dependency/browser versions and prerequisites → cwd + exact command/input or appropriate domain check → expected versus observed output/exit/result + actual result artifact → executed-pass | executed-fail | pending-unrun | blocked → supported | contradicted | unverified → limits + gap owner/next action`.

Use relevant fields, explicit not-applicable reasons and equivalent terse sourced prose for a tiny/domain check; do not force a software matrix on a paragraph or trip. An artifact must contain the actual check observation, not a copied producer PASS/result JSON. Attempt completion, a command's successful exit, criterion support and verified unit state are distinct: a completed partial report or passing static/build check cannot absorb a required unrun runtime criterion. Missing required tools/prerequisites leave that criterion pending/blocked and unverified with owner/action; do not install automatically, weaken acceptance or substitute static inspection, HTTP, mocks or screenshots for required real framework/browser execution. Test-plan/review approval is not execution.

**Executable teaching is code even in Markdown:** route published examples, exercises and solutions through `verify-code`. The verifier independently reconstructs student-visible files/support/imports/exports/entry point and actual solution edits/commands in a clean isolated own-attempt copy using the published pinned runtime/framework/dependencies and relevant actual browser. Establish requirements/primary-source expectations before inspecting the solution; use meaningful normal/error/recovery/counterexample and affected regression checks with independently derived expected values. Execute the published example and the actual edited solution, not just the producer's working screen. Missing instructions, wrong solutions or incompatible pinned versions are findings, not permission for private fixes, extra scaffolding, silent upgrades or mock proof. Preserve original failed target/results; only its producer may publish a repaired new stable target for a new distinct verifier invocation. Required real-browser geometry uses the full `verify-code` rendered method; screenshots/build/HTTP cannot replace it. No universal TDD or broad suite for prose.

**Non-executable domain checks** use appropriate primary sources and concrete audience/terminology/continuity, scenario, feasibility/transfer and arithmetic observations. Identify passage/source dates, route/quantity/currency and applicability as relevant, expected versus observed facts/calculations and limits. Document inspection cannot establish an executed trip, current availability from stale extracts or a reader study. Keep executable correctness, technical prose correctness, aesthetic/expert hypotheses, actual preference and learner/user effectiveness separate; an approved effectiveness claim needs fitting actual participant/task/method/results evidence, never an inferred study from code passing.

The verifier writes a **separate concise user outcome summary** in its own attempt, distinct from detailed report and compact receipt: exact target/unit/revision, actual investigator/checker identities, what actually ran, material criterion status, primary proof/report links, limits and bounded next action. The orchestrator obtains a successful compact receipt, confirms accessible report/identity, then **actually reads and substantively relays this exact successful summary artifact** through the entire existing discussion/answer route **before an authoritative outcome state update**. Retain its stable artifact identity and actual read/relay reference; stored unseen summaries, expanded receipts and transcripts do not satisfy visibility. Missing/inconsistent receipt, absent report/result or missing promised summary triggers a new distinct assigned worker recovery or an honest blocker, never an invented PASS. A successful receipt may describe a completed check with failed/unverified criteria; relay those statuses truthfully. This summary reports current checked outcomes, not a promise of future reconciliation. Reconciliation remains the assigned writer's separate task under the existing authority rules.

## Artifact-grounded authoritative reconciliation

Assign **one sole authoritative writer** and exact owned brief/decision paths for reconciliation. Supply the current unit/approved revision, required criteria/subclaims and applicable requirements/D/current review statements/version/response; accessible own-unit investigator/applicability; actual producer invocation/report/compact receipt/deliverable and target/baseline/source manifests; actual distinct independent verifier invocation/report/compact receipt/result artifacts and separate short checked-outcome summary. Include the summary's actual read/relay reference per Independent outcome proof. Receipts route work; they do not replace primary artifacts. The writer **actually opens** the assigned primary files and current output/authority, reusing the [criterion proof trace](#independent-outcome-proof-and-user-visibility) by link rather than copying it into another tracker.

For each required criterion/subclaim, independently compare that proof to the actual current approved basis and output/baseline manifest: unit/revision/producer/stable target/published variant, real invocation identity/independence, source applicability, independent expected result, method, environment/prerequisites, actual execution/observation/result artifact and limits. Do not adopt producer/verifier conclusions without this comparison. Report accessibility/existence and correct current criterion support are **distinct** checks. Distinguish **genuinely absent** files from accessible **wrong-unit/revision/target/baseline**, incomplete/partial reports, pending-unrun/blocked checks and contradictory primary observations (including a summary PASS against failed results). Give each affected criterion its supported/contradicted/unverified disposition and bounded owner/action; attempt `done`, exit 0 and a filename establish no blanket verification.

Valid applicable current primary evidence may correct stale historical “no report” or pending narrative through the assigned writer. Retain the original then-pending snapshot/reference and explain the current replacement basis; never invent a run, backfill original completion or rerun solely because checking finished. Required pending/unverified/contradicted items prevent whole-unit `verified`. Passing partials cover the whole only if the **actual approved criterion basis** supports that coverage. A fallback needs actual scoped approval plus the exact **actually executed** variant/evidence/limits and covers only its applicable criteria; no invented exclusion/fallback, weakened criterion or impossible historical audit. Unknown/conflicting meaningful source authority stops affected claims for upstream resolution.

Failure/mismatch leads to bounded producer-owned repair, additional check, blocker, renewed same-unit investigation or return to the affected decision scope. Preserve failed/incomplete/replaced reports, results and rationale, replacement links and impacted units. Changed output requires a **newly named stable target and actual new distinct independent scoped check**, including necessary affected interactions/regressions; moving sources invalidate applicability to the current target, not historical results about the old bytes. Recheck only changed or unresolved concerns, not a broader suite merely because verification completed. Material uncovered uncertainty renews distinct own-unit investigation with retained/revalidated evidence. Material outcome/boundary/acceptance/inherited changes require the matching revision/D-scope/checkpoint gates, pausing affected work while unrelated authorized branches continue.

The assigned writer **actually updates** owned criterion/evidence dispositions and links, state, complete meaningful established/proposed Decision log from actual source authority and worker choices, reasoned current Work split, retained history/impacts and one bounded next action **before representing the brief as current/complete or the outcome as reconciled**. A recommendation, report or receipt alone is incomplete; a non-writer hands off needed edits without creating another authority store. Preserve actual approvals and superseded rationale; editorial current-evidence correction supplies no new scope approval. Use the existing Decision logs/Work split methods, not a duplicate field schema.

Assign a separately owned **concise reconciliation summary** referencing the actual updated authority and primary proof, remaining criterion limits and next action. Through the entire existing orchestrator discussion/answer route, obtain its successful compact receipt, confirm report access/identity, then actually read/substantively relay that exact summary and retain its stable identity/read-relay reference; missing/inconsistent handoff follows the existing distinct worker recovery or honest blocker. Do not duplicate authority, expand receipts or make the earlier verifier summary claim later state edits. A verified child supplies evidence only: the parent needs its **own independent coverage/coherence/integration check against parent criteria and actual criterion reconciliation**. Assign that parent check after the selected outcomes settle; child checks/source edits do not satisfy it or preselect a further child.

## Consequential decisions

Create a `D-...` record for a choice whose reversal would materially affect approved scope, other units, or an inherited constraint; keep routine reversible implementation choices in the unit's report/brief. Each record has question, drivers, alternatives, evidence (with uncertainty), choice/rationale, consequences, applicability, approval and related decision IDs. An approved record applies to the whole named root endeavor, to a named subtree, or to explicitly named units; it is not automatically project-wide across unrelated roots. Children inherit applicable decisions. Verify assumptions briefly before acting; reopen deeply only for conflicting evidence, changed constraints, or a significant better alternative.

Workers flag a conflict and stop affected work; they do not silently override an approved decision. A scoped exception is a separately approved decision referring to the general decision and the exceptional units; the general decision stays active outside them. A superseding decision retains and links the original rationale and lists the impacted units to review. A newer or more local decision has no automatic precedence: the explicit approved exception/supersession relationship determines it. Update both records' links when approved. If evidence needed to justify a lasting decision lives only in a disposable attempt report, preserve or summarize it in the durable decision and reference stable sources.

## Decision logs

Every new/prepared brief and every prospectively materially revised brief must contain a `Decision log`; this does not require retrofitting historical briefs. The log is a readable summary, not another authority or status store. Include **all meaningful established applicable decisions**: user outcome, boundary, constraint or preference positions; inherited root/protocol constraints and approved D choices; and settled delegated local approach, check, split/leaf or dependency choices. Identify each actual position and its applicability; a generic ancestor link or D-links-only list is insufficient. Group coherent entries only when their distinct positions, statuses and sources remain identifiable. Keep it concise, not a microaction journal. `None established` is valid only when genuinely no meaningful decisions are established, not when supplied requirements or inherited constraints were omitted.

Use separate **established decisions** and **proposals/open alternatives** tables (see `unit.md`). Each meaningful established entry states its question/position, category/status, applicability, alternatives, short rationale, and source/evidence/**actual authority**. Distinguish established user requirements, inherited constraints, approved applicable D choices and delegated settled local choices; a worker choice is not user agreement. Proposals, open questions and unapproved consequential records remain separate, with their scope, evidence gaps and next decision/action. A checkpoint authorizes its named revision, not every proposal within it; a chosen check method is not an executed or successful check.

Use recorded historical alternatives and rationale only. Where absent, write `unknown/not recorded`; label any **newly considered/proposed** comparison and present worker rationale/inference as such. Do not invent earlier user deliberation or motives. Link actual user requests/review, applicable ancestor clauses, worker reports or D/checkpoint records at the scope they support; describe the meaningful inherited positions rather than merely saying “follow parent.” Missing or conflicting material inherited authority/applicability leaves that coverage unresolved and stops affected claims/work for upstream resolution.

Consequential D records and checkpoints remain authoritative. A D summary links its approval reference, scoped applicability and any **explicit approved** exception/supersession relationship to the authoritative records; preserve original positions/rationale, replacement links and impacted units to review. An exception leaves the general decision effective elsewhere. A newer or more local summary has no precedence and cannot approve, override or repair authority by itself. Follow the consequential-decision conflict and bidirectional-link rules above, with one assigned record writer; renewed evidence alone grants no approval.

One assigned planner/state writer creates and maintains the authoritative brief/log, consuming actual sources and meaningful worker-choice reports at preparation/reconciliation updates **before representing the brief as current/complete**. Other workers report settled meaningful choices, rejected alternatives, proposals and changed evidence with status, alternatives, concise rationale, sources and scope for that writer; report-only workers gain no brief ownership. Distinguish recommendations/findings from established choices, retain superseded rationale/relationships and flag invalidated positions. Sequential ownership transfer is allowed; simultaneous log writers are not. Editorial log/link maintenance alone does not bump material revision; actual outcome/boundary/criterion or consequential inherited changes do and require matching authorization. Routine reversible choices need no microapproval or new phase checkpoint, and choice handoffs need no duplicated log tracker.

## Attempts and handoff

The portable role files are `.agents/roles/orchestrator.md`, `investigator.md`, `planner.md`, `producer.md`, and `verifier.md`. The OpenCode agent names are `workflow-orchestrator`, `workflow-investigator`, `workflow-planner`, `workflow-producer`, and `workflow-verifier` respectively; **do not** infer a role filename from an OpenCode agent name. OpenCode loads the corresponding prompt through its configuration. A worker need not reread its own role file unless its prompt was not loaded.

Every invocation has a distinct ID and writes `.workflow/attempts/<unit-id>/<attempt-id>/report.md`. The caller supplies unit/brief revision (or initial-root framing basis), role, chosen skill, bounded assignment, inputs, owned output paths, attempt path, and what to stop/escalate for; downstream assignments include the linked applicable own-unit investigator report and any revalidation/replacement basis. An investigator or verifier writes only its attempt files; a planner may be assigned an authoritative brief/decision as the sole writer; a producer owns its named deliverables and its attempt files. A verifier checks a stable, named output or attempt, not a moving target. Reports describe observations, evidence/source paths, inferences, unknowns, work performed, checks/results, conflicts, and next step. Use `.agents/workflow/attempt.md` as a starting point.

Include meaningful choice/proposal handoff detail in that report for the assigned brief writer per Decision logs, not a second authoritative log or an expanded final receipt. If no meaningful choice arose, say so proportionately. The writer reads the linked reports to update the current summary; this does not replace the orchestrator's compact receipt/owned answer-artifact discussion route.

Within the existing assignment inputs, include available reader/audience and expertise context, the current problem, requested depth and definitions already introduced in reader-visible material, with source/context links. Do not invent known terms or treat internal reports as reader-visible context. Require full loading of `critical-reasoning` and `clear-communication` for every role, including the orchestrator; the latter is the canonical communication procedure, not a separate state store.

The worker's final message to the orchestrator must contain only: **unit ID; attempt ID; status** (`done`, `blocked`, `needs-decision`, `failed`); **key finding** (1–2 sentences); **decision needed** (or `none`); **report and deliverable paths**. Do not paste logs, detailed evidence, or transcripts. If the worker cannot write the report, return `failed` with the reason and do not claim completion. The orchestrator consumes this bounded receipt; a successor worker reads the report, not the orchestrator's transcript. Instruction-based handoffs are a v1 hypothesis to measure in trials, not a guarantee enforced by a plugin.

Per-attempt reports may be gitignored/local, but do not delete one while another agent is using it or while it is the only evidence for an unresolved check. Keep briefs, checkpoints, consequential decisions, and lasting deliverables in the endeavor workspace. The writer who updates an authoritative brief links the relevant attempt/check artifact and records the next action. Avoid mirrored status trackers; add a small `.workflow/active.md` navigation index only if finding the right root becomes difficult, and do not make it authoritative.

## Resuming and parallel work

From the user's named unit/issue, locate its root under `.workflow/units/`; if ambiguous, ask. Read the unit brief, its parent checkpoint (or the root's own), applicable decisions from the ancestor chain and any explicit named-unit decisions, and only the referenced evidence needed now. Check that approved revisions still match and apply the Unit entry evidence/reuse gate. Never reconstruct authority from chat alone. On a new root, arrange bounded distinct investigation then framing before the first checkpoint.

Parallel investigation may produce separate attempt reports. Parallel production is allowed only with disjoint owned outputs and resolved dependencies; sequence overlapping writes. Record the exact output/attempt being independently checked. Changed decisions pause only dependent work. Checking can mean tests, inspection, review against a manuscript brief, or verification of a travel constraint; select a method that could actually detect failure.
